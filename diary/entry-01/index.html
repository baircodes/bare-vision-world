Holiday — Green Day

~~Merry Christmas!~~ ~~Merry Christmas.~~ ~~Merry Christmas..?~~ **recycling Cadi Heron’s line in Mean Girls** ***‘’*It’s December 25th’’** This is how the 2025 holiday season feels like for my ego. My Christmases haven’t been internally ‘’child-like merry‘’ since the 2009 edition. The difference is this year, I don’t feel sorry for myself: I finally digested what my family means by ‘’Christmas is for the Children’’. I’ve already spent 15 years mourning versions of reality that could never* come true. Now that I am reflecting back, those are versions of myself that… I never actually wanted to become in the first place. I mean, I thought I did for like all of my life prior to now. So, whenever it’s time to sit down and face my soul, to cleanse the static and see clearly… I came to realize that the reality that men + women in my demography envision through the digital sphere, through social media, is not the atmosphere I want to curate for myself. Of course, some of the content I see can inspire me and speak to me. I simply don’t want to live someone else’s life. I’ve always been hellbent on curating my own world. For the longest time it did make me so sad and nostalgic to see online, because I thought, I too still desired the seemingly happy family gatherings at Christmas. Mountains of food. The fun games. The aesthetic pictures. Making memories. Mostly because…That used to be my ego’s reality. I remember the feeling. That’s *precisely* where sales, marketing, neurobiology, and social media intersect; together, they align forces to overstimulate human beings’ nervous system, to ingeniously plants seeds of what feelings to market, sell and hook society on 24/7, 365 baby. All gas no breaks. *@my-loved-ones reading this, First of all ily besties welcome to my brain frfr, secondly do you get the importance of my atypical smartphone habits now?* So, what do all of those above stated actions^^ have in common? Connection. But connection isn’t tied to Christmas. It isn’t tied to family only, nor is it tied to presents, pictures, shared meals and boxing day. Space needs to be created for genuine connection to strengthen, which includes the 364 other Gregorian calendar (🥱) days of the year. Christmas, or X Holiday, is just the permission slip most ***believe they need*** in order to gather together and love each other. And that’s why it makes December a great month of distraction for…let’s globally call them Earth’s current ‘’highly disillusioned decision makers’’, to make some of the most desperately lame (*once you zoom out enough*) late stage capitalism butterfly effect moves that have the power to influence the next decade on a global scale. Exhibit A: [Netflix vs Paramount vs Warner Bros](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H_03T6JNx-I). And they aren’t covering any of it. Society is just too lazy and distracted to connect the information dots themselves, critically think and claim back their sovereignty. I’m not saying to obsess over it. But like dude, if that’s you, please make an effort. Haven’t you seen [Idiocracy](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/idiocracy)? Its 20 year anniversary is in 2026. Crazy, **I know**. As I currently am in the final year of closing the gap between my 20s and my 30s, my personal daily perception lens has massively increased in clarity and focus. I’d be lying if I said this wasn’t years in the making: Deprogramming yourself doesn’t happen overnight. It happens gradually until you make the decision to choose daily awareness and claim sovereignty over your internal ecosystem. Aka, once you have faced yourself with open arms and a smile instead of yielding out your sword. Aka somatically locking in self-trust within your curated world. Self-security is the ground zero that leads you towards freedom. Today, what I currently analyze is that, the known singularity, *the flaw*, with the world’s [current ethics](https://www.jsc-chambers.co.uk/post/utilitarianism-the-nihilistic-philosophy-that-threatens-freedom-and-the-rule-of-law)** behind decision making highlights what late stage capitalism leaders childishly refuse to [clock in](https://www.reddit.com/r/popculturechat/comments/1lck06k/youre_not_getting_it_its_not_clocking_to_you_its/): The metrics used to *accurately* calculate sustainable long term positive value can **NOT** and should **NOT** be quantifiable. In other words, it is ironically what so many have been promoting through art and song: we simply cannot put a price tag on pleasure/happiness. And the concept of ‘’greater good’’? That’s highly subjective and can be twisted in so many ways as long as it fits seemingly well somewhere in the narrative (*& that’s on Hollywood PR for dummies*).

SO WITH ALL OF THIS BEING SAID. Here I am I guess? Through these sporadic digital diary entries, sharing pieces of my own sacred codex, with the intention of nudging the parts of the reader’s cortex that harbors their dormant curiosity and creativity. I’m literally personifying MSN’s iconic wizz feature (IYKYK). *DAPH SENT YOU A WIZZ!* Wake up from your digital trance, go out there, explore the world and make art. Tomorrow, before checking out your digital shopping carts, try to take a deep awareness breath and fully render yourself back into your natural landscape Take awareness on how technology has had the power to physically *suck you in*. Is this purchase bringing you closer to who you are meant to embody, or is it quenching your thirst for immediate gratification? You hold the power to consciously break the spell. All you need to do is… choose the atypical behaviour. Don’t wait for the glitch in the Matrix; you are the glitch. Bisous D x
