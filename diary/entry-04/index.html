So It Goes — Mac Miller

The Codes Are Always Coding

Wooaaah. Hi.

A lot is moving globally right now.

I’m feeling grounded in discernment. Flexible in thought, intentional about how i let information enter my field of awareness.

Some days, it’s more challenging to find fun.

Other days, I remember it effortlessly. In the most random places. I train myself to stay available to that current.

Regardless, I stay curious about how I feel, and how that feeling interacts with the world around me.

So it goes.

As long as I can freely be myself, nourish + move my body, breathe deeply, and return to presence, I’m genuinely thankful.

I was raised to be in tuned with the signal of the present moment.

& then life happened.

It made me stronger. Wiser. Softer.

For the past few years, I’ve been rediscovering the enjoyment of simplicity. In my experience, those are the real anchors. They pull me back to now.

And I’ve always known that’s where I prefer to exist.

As much as I enjoy material design and aesthetics, I’m experiential at my core.

Life is an adventure.

A world to discover.

And I intend to explore it. Externally & internally.

Yes, it’s beautiful to build: nurturing family + relationships, creating a career, designing physical + digital landscapes.

What I return to most is how I feel while creating them.

Comfort through chaos.

Breathing through tightness.

Heartship.

I’ve learned to curate safety in my own nervous system. A super chill system, if you will. I don’t want my energy field to add unnecessary pressure to any environment.

We’re both Sending & Receiving.

Pulling & Pushing.

Attracting & Resisting.

You can be conscious about your frequency — or unconscious of the way it shapes your environment.

For me, maintaining energetic integrity is easier in solitude or within small circles I trust.

In larger groups, I often observe competition running quietly in the background. I’ve opted out of that script.

I remember being 11 or 12, competing in figure skating. I cared about performing well, yes — but more than that, I cared about enjoying the performance and cheering for my friends.

I’ve never let competition poison my joy.

I might stumble.

But I don’t surrender my rhythm.

At 29, that feels clearer than ever.

Detachment, for me, isn’t avoidance.

It’s loving without possession.

It’s choosing sovereignty over control.

Push, Pull. Surrender.

The codes are always coding.

And lately, I’ve been thinking about how much of society is emotionally entrained without realizing it.

Music codes us faster than almost anything.

Rhythm.

Repetition.

Hooks.

Chorus.

You don’t even notice when your nervous system syncs.

Commercial pop.

Western dominance.

Algorithmic loops.

It’s not inherently wrong, but it is influential.

EDM feels different to me.

More universal.

More wordless.

More collective.

Certain artists carry frequency that regulates instead of agitates.

The point isn’t to avoid certain vibrations in fear.

The point is to be conscious of what you allow to run on repeat in your system.

Override is possible.

Pause before reacting.

Breathe before speaking.

Step away from the digital field.

Refuse scarcity competition.

It’s coexistence — intuition and intention.

Feminine and Masculine.

Flow and Structure.

I don’t reject the world.

I simply refuse to be unconsciously coded by it.

Recently, I watched the internet mobilize compassion for a baby monkey.

It struct me. Not because it was dramatic. But because it proved something.

Empathy isn’t dead.

It can activate instantly.

Virality can direct feeling.

Humans still crave innocence.

Still feel collectively.

Still remember love, even if briefly.

That matters.

The real cost of unconscious coding isn’t apocalypse.

It’s shallowness.

Lack of depth.

Lack of sustainability.

Lack of meaning across generations.

When we don’t understand the mechanics shaping us, connection weakens.

When we do, it strengthens.

To many, I appear composed.

Resilient.

Steady.

To myself, I’ve cried oceans.

Doubted.

Shamed.

Questioned.

Neither defines me.

Both refined me.

I don’t fear being misunderstood anymore.

I fear losing my internal steadiness.

So I protect it.

Steward it.

Train it.

Like a fish swimming through shifting currents.

The surface can be chaotic.

Underneath, there’s always a deeper current.

That’s where I’m chilling.

The codes are always coding.

But so am I.
